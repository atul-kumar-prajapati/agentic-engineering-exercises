# REV-482: Recognized revenue mismatch

Status: Open
Severity: High
Opened: 2026-08-09

## Request

Correct recognized-revenue totals in the dashboard and scheduled snapshot. Use the current metric rules and billing-account boundaries, preserve gross-volume behaviour, and reject events without a valid account mapping.

## Reported behaviour

- Credits are still counted as recognized revenue.
- Refunds increase rather than reduce the total.
- Two tenants belonging to one billing account appear as separate totals.
- The dashboard and scheduled snapshot can disagree after separate fixes.

## Scope

The incident is limited to the shared recognized-revenue calculation. Gross volume, public consumer result shapes, and the tenant-to-account directory must remain compatible.
