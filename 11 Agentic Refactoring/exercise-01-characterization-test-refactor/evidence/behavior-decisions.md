## Classification summary

All seven observed behaviors are classified **preserve**. Four carry suspected-bug follow-ups (support-override discount zeroing, negative payment-history acceptance, misleading `plan-not-supported` on mature pro accounts, absent input validation). Per `docs/legacy-behavior-notes.md` and the exercise README, none of these was fixed during the refactor; the payment-history and support-override quirks in particular are load-bearing for current callers and must only change behind an approved, separately reviewed behavior change.
